Projects is where we keep any general information about ongoing PRISMA topics. These should have a general description (preferably public-accessible) for what we're doing. Make sure to include any researchers involved as authors. 

You may wish to link your repository and publications to your project. Use the article shortcode to add any relevant posts in the blog to your project (/posts/your_publication/). Preferably, do not host publications under the projects page. If there is something very relevant to your project that should be permanently linked to it, such as documentations, then it can be added under the /projects/ address.
